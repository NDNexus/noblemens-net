Benefits of Natural Perfume

The Experience of Natural Aroma.

Human mind is irritated by noise, bad smell, annoying things, pollution etc. On the contrary, naturally occurring aromas do the opposite.

Perfume enters lung and then goes to blood stream. When applied directly on skin, it enters body through absorption. Blood carries it to various parts of the body. Many aromatic substances cross ‘Blood-brain-barrier’ and enter the central nervous system (brain). It is where natural perfumes do wonder.

It elevates a person’s mind to heavenly ecstasy. It is anti-depressant and anti-anxiety. And so many positive things start to appear.

Recent scientific studies suggest that the fragrances produce immediate changes in physiological parameters such as blood pressure, muscle tension, pupil dilation, skin temperature, pulse rate and brain activity.

Measurement of brain activity by Electroencephalographic response (EEG) reveals that natural perfume improves mental condition.

Jasmine oil arouses change in brain waves that is associated with the increase of positive emotions such as the feeling of well-being, or feeling active, fresh and romantic. Sandalwood oil causes changes in brain electrical wave which bring soothing, calming and comforting effect.

Reference: Article in ‘Scientia Pharmaceutica’, Ref 2016, 84, 724–751.

Evils of Synthetic Perfume

Why We Chose a Different Direction.

In today’s world, big industrial houses are involved in developing and marketing synthetic aromas. Artificial chemicals based fragrance invaded almost entire day to day items like bathing soaps, washing powder, even food items like fruit juice, ice cream, soft drink etc. The list is endless.

A good smelling chemical is synthesized in laboratory, but what about its evil and toxic effect on health?

A study shows that about 35% of the population exposed to artificial fragrant material reported health problems, such as respiratory disorders, mucosal symptoms, migraine headaches, skin problems, asthma attacks, neurological, cognitive, gastrointestinal problems, cardiovascular problems, immune system problems, musculoskeletal problems and others.

Ref. Paper published in ‘Air Quality Atmosphere and Health’ (2016) 9:861–866.

Synthetic Fragrance

A single synthetic perfume brand available in market may contain anything up to 500 mixture of chemicals.

Ingredients

Except the scent manufacturer, nobody knows the list and dangers involved in these chemicals are not available.

Disclosure

Manufacturers are not bound by any law to reveal the ingredients.

While mechanism like regulatory body and government laws exist for food and drugs around the world to protect general population against hazardous chemical and unscrupulous manufacturer, no such thing exists in case of perfume. Manufacturers are not bound by any law to reveal the ingredients.

Commercial interests make things more complex. In regulated fields too, consumers are not always on the safe side. See for yourself … artificial colour / flavour are added in processed food for mere visual appeal. It contributes nothing to health. Some of them are banned in several other countries, but still allowed in India.

How & Where to Apply

Apply a Small Quantity Directly on Skin.

Many people first put some perfume on their palm or on back of the palm, then rub it on clothes – Do not do this.

Apply a small quantity directly on skin using the applicator rod.

Applicator rod is the plastic rod or stick which is inseparable part of the bottle closure plug.

Recommended locations

(i) Skin on bare chest. Perfume lasts longer at this location.

(ii) Behind ear lobe.

(iii) On the neck, below chin.

The rationale behind these suggested locations

These points are near to nose.

Does not gets washed away while washing or wiping the face.

A simple way to apply

Apply a small quantity directly on skin using the applicator rod. Avoid rubbing the perfume onto clothes.
