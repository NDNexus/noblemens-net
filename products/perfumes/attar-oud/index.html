Traditionally Inspired, Naturally Crafted

Oud has been treasured for centuries for its distinctive fragrance, depth and character. Agarwood develops its aromatic resins within the wood, which are carefully processed to produce precious Oud oil.

From Agarwood to Oud Oil

Traditionally, Oud oil is extracted from Agarwood through careful soaking and distillation using water and gentle heat. The aromatic oil is then separated and collected, preserving the natural character of the wood.

For our blend, we source naturally distilled Agarwood oil and carefully combine it with complementary natural oils to create a distinctive fragrance of our own. The result is a naturally inspired Oud that stays true to the character of its precious source.

A Fragrance That Lingers

Oud is not a fragrance that simply announces itself and disappears. Its character develops gradually, revealing layers of warm, sweet and woody depth as it settles on the skin.

With its gentle sandalwood character and rich Oud foundation, the fragrance can linger for approximately 4–6 hours, and may remain much longer when the skin is not washed.

A small amount is enough to create a distinctive personal scent — rich, composed and effortlessly timeless.

How to Use

A small amount of Oud is all you need. Apply it directly to clean, bare skin or use a cotton swab for more precise application.

  • Behind the ears: Apply gently to the folds of the ear for a subtle, lasting fragrance.
  • On skin: Apply to pulse points such as the wrists or neck, or anywhere you prefer.
  • On clothes: First apply the Oud to your skin, then gently rub the scented skin against your clothing. Avoid applying it directly to fabric, as the natural oils may cause staining.
  • For longer wear: Apply to areas that are not frequently washed, allowing the fragrance to linger naturally.
